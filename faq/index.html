01Where is Okie Kids Learning Academy located?

The listed address is 3720 S. 113th W. Ave, Sand Springs, OK 74063. You can open directions on the Contact page.

02What ages does the center serve?

The Oklahoma Human Services listing includes infants (0–11 months), toddlers (12–23 months), preschoolers (2–4 years), and school-age children (5 years and older).

03What are the center’s hours?

Oklahoma Human Services lists Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM–5:30 PM. Saturday and Sunday are listed as closed. Call to confirm holiday schedules.

04Does Okie Kids accept childcare subsidies?

Yes. The Oklahoma Human Services provider listing identifies this center as accepting child care subsidy assistance. Contact the center and the state program to discuss your family’s eligibility and enrollment.

05Is the program licensed?

The center is listed by Oklahoma Human Services under child care license K830058381. The state portal currently lists a 3-star program level and a capacity of 50 children.

06How can I ask about enrollment or tuition?

Call (661) 675-7614 or email joylynn1982@hotmail.com. Tuition rates and real-time openings are not published in the source record, so ask the center for current details.

07Is transportation available?

Transportation and school pickup arrangements are not confirmed in the state record. Contact the center to ask about availability, routes, and policies.

08How can I see more about the center?

You can visit the center’s Instagram and Facebook profiles linked below, or reach the team directly. The official Oklahoma Human Services record is also available online.
