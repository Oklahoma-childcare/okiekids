✳A HELPFUL STARTING POINT

Before you enroll,
ask away.

Each center has its own policies. These questions can help you understand what a typical day might look like for your child.

✳◡✦
01

What does a typical day look like?

Ask about play, learning activities, snacks, meals, rest time, and age-specific routines.

02

What should my child bring?

Confirm supplies, labeled clothing, diapers or wipes, and any required forms.

03

How do you communicate with families?

Learn how daily updates, special announcements, and questions are handled.

04

What are the enrollment steps?

Ask about current openings, fees, subsidy processing, and required paperwork.

05

What are the drop-off and pick-up rules?

Confirm accepted arrival times, authorized pickups, and attendance policies.
